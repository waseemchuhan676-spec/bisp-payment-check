How to Check Your Next Survey

Find out whether you need a new BISP Dynamic Survey (NSER) and when to get surveyed.

Steps to check your next survey

  1. Send your CNIC to 8171 — the reply tells you your current survey and eligibility status.
  2. If the reply says a survey is required (or you have never been surveyed), visit your nearest BISP tehsil office.
  3. Bring your original CNIC — the survey is done at the office, free of charge.
  4. After the survey, wait for the 8171 SMS about eligibility — do not trust a message from anyone else.
Check Status with CNIC

Important to know

  • There is no single nationwide "next survey date" — the Dynamic Survey runs on an ongoing basis at BISP tehsil offices, so you can get surveyed any time.
  • If you were surveyed before, BISP may ask for a re-survey when records are updated — the 8171 SMS will tell you.
  • There is no online survey form — anyone offering "online survey registration" for a fee is committing fraud.
  • Survey and registration are completely free.

Important information

Official SMS Code

8171

Send your CNIC only to this number — never to any other number.

Official Portal

8171.bisp.gov.pk

This is the only official website for checking online.

BISP Helpline

0800-26477

Call free for complaints or information.

Benazir Kafaalat Installment

Rs 14,500

The installment paid every three months to registered women.

Beware of fraud

BISP payments and surveys are completely free — never pay anyone a fee or commission, and never share your PIN/OTP, to check, get surveyed, or withdraw your installment. Trust payment news only from SMS received from 8171.
