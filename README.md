# BISP Payment Check (8171)

Simple one-page site to help Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) beneficiaries check their Kafaalat payment.

- Enter your 13-digit CNIC number (auto-formatted as XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X).
- Tap **Payment Check Karein** to open an SMS to **8171** with your CNIC ready, or open the official **8171 web portal** (8171.bisp.gov.pk).
- The CNIC is never saved or sent anywhere by this page — it stays on your device.

Facts on the page: Kafaalat installment Rs 14,500 · official helpline 0800-26477 · only 8171 is official — never pay anyone a fee or share your PIN.

This page does not check payments itself. Payment status comes only from the official 8171 SMS service or the 8171 web portal.
