Is Website Ke Baare Mein

Ye website kya hai, kis liye hai, aur is ki had kya hai.

Ye website kya hai?

Ye aik ghair-sarkari (unofficial) helper website hai jo aam logon ko BISP payment aur survey check karne ka official tareeqa samjhane ke liye banayi gayi hai. Is ka BISP ya Government of Pakistan se koi talluq nahi hai.

Ye website kya karti hai?

  • Aap ka CNIC number le kar 8171 par SMS bhejne ya official portal kholne mein madad karti hai.
  • BISP survey check karne, qist aur helpline ki maloomat aik jagah deti hai.
  • Fraud se bachne ki rehnumai deti hai — sirf 8171 par bharosa karein.

Ye website kya NAHI karti?

  • Ye khud aap ki payment check nahi karti — is ke paas BISP ka koi database ya record nahi hai.
  • Ye registration ya survey nahi karti — survey sirf BISP tehsil office mein hota hai.
  • Ye payment nahi deti aur na hi ehliyat ka faisla karti hai.

Aap ka CNIC mehfooz hai

Jo CNIC number aap is site par likhte hain, wo sirf aap ke apne browser mein rehta hai — SMS ya portal kholne ke liye istemal hota hai. Wo kahin save, upload ya share nahi hota. Is ki tafseel Privacy Policy mein hai.

Official zariye

  • SMS: apna CNIC 8171 par bhej dein.
  • Portal: 8171.bisp.gov.pk
  • Helpline: 0800-26477 (muft call).

Is site par ads dikhaye jate hain — in se site chalane mein madad milti hai. Ads ka matlab ye nahi ke site BISP ki official site hai.
