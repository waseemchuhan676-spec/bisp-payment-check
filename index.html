Important information

Benazir Kafaalat Installment

Rs 14,500

The installment paid every three months to registered women.

BISP Helpline

0800-26477

Call free for complaints or information.

Official SMS Code

8171

Send your CNIC only to this number — never to any other number.

Official Portal

8171.bisp.gov.pk

This is the only official website for checking online.

Beware of fraud

BISP payments are completely free — never pay anyone a fee or commission, and never share your PIN/OTP, to check or withdraw your installment. Trust payment news only from SMS received from 8171.

BISP Payment Check is an unofficial helper website. It does not check payments itself — the official payment reply comes only from 8171 SMS or the official portal.
