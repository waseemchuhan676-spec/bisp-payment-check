Taleemi Wazaif — Children's Education Stipend

Check your children's Benazir Taleemi Wazaif stipend through 8171 SMS or the official portal.

How to check the children's stipend

  1. Use the mother's CNIC — the Taleemi Wazaif stipend is linked to the mother's CNIC. Send her 13-digit CNIC number to 8171 by SMS.
  2. Read the reply from 8171 — it includes the Taleemi Wazaif status along with the Kafaalat payment information.
  3. If the reply does not mention the stipend — check at your nearest BISP tehsil office or call the helpline 0800-26477 for free.
  4. Confirm the amount — the stipend amount depends on the child's class level. Confirm the exact amount through 8171 or at the BISP office.
Check with Mother's CNIC

Who can get it, and how to enrol

  • For the children of women registered in Benazir Kafaalat.
  • The child must be enrolled in school.
  • Enrolment is done at the BISP office — bring the mother's original CNIC, the child's B-Form (CRC), and proof of school admission.
  • Children must keep attending school (70% attendance is required to keep receiving the stipend).
  • Enrolment and the stipend are completely free — never pay anyone a fee for it.
